Good software gets
out of the way.

The best interfaces make complex things feel simple. They help us focus on what matters, then quietly step aside.

Think about the tools you return to every day. They rarely demand your attention. Instead, they create space for an idea, a conversation, or a small discovery.

Reading across languages should work the same way. A translation can preserve the meaning and the rhythm of the original while remaining a natural part of the page.

Preserve the details

A link to further reading should remain a link. Emphasis should stay where it belongs. Technical expressions like f(x) = x² should survive the journey unchanged.

Good design removes the friction between intention and action.
example.ts
const reading = {
  language: "ru",
  preserveFormatting: true,
};

Follow the reader

Long pages don't need to be translated all at once. Start with the paragraphs in view, then continue as the reader scrolls. Keep the rest ready for later.
